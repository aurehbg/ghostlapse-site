Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Booflip is a timelapse camera app. Everything it makes stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud.

  • No account, no server of our own.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • The developer never receives your photos or any data about you.

Photos

Your shots are stored as JPEG files inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. If “Also save to Photos” is on for a timelapse (it is by default and can be turned off per timelapse), a copy of each shot is also saved to an album named “Booflip · <timelapse name>” in your Photo Library.

Booflip reads your Photo Library only to show your photos while you pick ones to import or a reference picture, and to keep its own album in order. If you don't allow access, the system photo picker is used instead and Booflip sees only the photos you pick.

Location

When you take a shot, Booflip records where it was taken. The spot of your first shot is saved so the camera can guide you back to it. This location is stored with the timelapse on your iPhone, and copies saved to Photos carry it like any camera photo.

You can move or forget the spot in the timelapse's settings, or deny location access entirely. The map shown in settings is provided by Apple Maps.

Face data

For face and body timelapses, Booflip finds your face using Apple's Vision framework, which runs entirely on your iPhone. From each shot it keeps only a few numbers: the position of the eyes, the box around the face, and the head angles. These numbers are used to line up the camera and your shots, and they are stored with the timelapse on your iPhone.

  • Face data is never shared or used to identify anyone. With iCloud sync on, it is copied to your private iCloud end-to-end encrypted, so only your devices can read it.
  • No face images, face templates or Face ID data are created or stored. The TrueDepth (depth) camera is not used.
  • Face data is deleted when you delete the timelapse or the app.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is on by default and can be turned off from the cloud button on the home screen. When it is on, your timelapses, shots and their settings are stored in your private iCloud database through Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

Only your Apple Account can access this data; the developer cannot. Face data, locations, and birth and due dates are end-to-end encrypted. Photos are protected by iCloud's standard encryption, or end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Face ID and passcode lock

You can lock a timelapse so its photos only show after Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. iOS performs the check; Booflip only learns whether it succeeded. The lock covers photos inside Booflip, not copies in your Photo Library.

Notifications

Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They contain only the timelapse's name and are not sent through any server.

Sharing

Booflip shares a photo, comparison or video only when you tap Share or Save, and only to the destination you choose.

Purchases

If you buy Booflip Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. Booflip only learns from Apple whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. It never sees your name, email or payment details.

Deleting your data

  • Delete a timelapse in its settings to remove its shots, location and face data from the app, and, with iCloud sync on, from iCloud and your other devices.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Copies saved to your Photo Library are kept; delete the “Booflip · …” album and its photos in the Photos app to remove them.

App data is included in your iPhone or iCloud backups if you have backups turned on, as with any app.

Children

Booflip does not collect data from anyone, including children. Photos of children taken with the app stay on your devices and in your own iCloud.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
